Kitchen Cabinets Cost in 2026: Stock vs Semi-Custom vs Custom

Kitchen cabinets are usually the biggest single line in a kitchen remodel, and the price range is enormous because the product itself is so different at each tier. The same 10-by-10 kitchen can cost a few thousand dollars or more than twenty thousand, depending on whether the boxes are stock, semi-custom, or fully custom.

According to Renovation Guide HQ's kitchen cabinets cost guide, stock cabinets run $2,000 to $8,000, semi-custom cabinets run $3,000 to $14,000, and custom cabinets run $10,000 to $24,000 for the same 10-by-10 layout, based on HomeGuide data. The per-linear-foot price, the industry's standard unit measuring the total width of your cabinet runs, ranges from roughly $100 per linear foot for stock up to $1,200 per linear foot for custom.

Stock cabinets are pre-manufactured in fixed sizes, usually 3-inch width increments, and sold off the shelf at Home Depot, Lowe's, and similar retailers. They are the right call when the budget is under $8,000, the timeline is tight, or the kitchen is a rental property. Semi-custom lines such as KraftMaid and Aristokraft start from stock platforms but let you modify dimensions, finishes, and features, with deeper drawers, pull-out shelving, specialty storage, and dozens of door styles.

Custom cabinets are built to order by a local shop or craftsman to your exact specifications: any size, any wood species, any finish, any interior configuration. That is what drives the top end of custom pricing. White oak runs $700 to $900 per linear foot installed, and walnut runs $800 to $1,000 per linear foot installed. Thermofoil and acrylic slabs cost $100 to $200 more per linear foot than painted MDF, and glass-front inserts add $50 to $150 per door, plus the cost of finishing the cabinet interior.

Refacing is the middle path worth pricing before you commit to new boxes. The guide notes refacing is mostly labor: the $150 to $450 per linear foot rate covers the refacing work itself, which at 20 linear feet is $3,000 to $9,000 for the rate alone, while the $5,000 to $13,000 all-in average for a full kitchen, per HomeGuide, also covers materials, hardware, and finishing. Moving plumbing, electrical, and gas lines can add $1,000 to $3,000 or more, so keep the layout if the budget is tight. When budgeting, apply your regional multiplier after selecting a cabinet type: an $8,000 semi-custom quote nationally becomes roughly $9,200 in Boston.
